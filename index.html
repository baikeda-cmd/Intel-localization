Intel: A story of Sustainability

Take a look at Intel's commitment to sustainability through time, and discover how that commitment has shaped a more sustainable for technology and the planet

Intel's sustainability journey

Explore each milestone by moving your pointer over a card.

1994

Building a better future

Formally began tracking and reporting on corporate responsibility and environmental metrics

2013

Smarter, more efficient chips

Achieved its goal of manufacturing the world's first "conflict-free" microprocessor (ensuring metals like tantalum were responsibly sourced)

2014

Reducing our footprint

Increased green power purchases to cover 100% of its projected U.S. electricity use for that year

2022

Innovating for net zero

Officially pledged to reach net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across its global operations (Scope 1 and 2) by 2040

2023

Improving energy efficiency

Reached 99% global renewable electricity usage. Sites in the U.S., Europe, Malaysia, Vietnam, and China hit 100% renewable power

2025

Lowering Waste

Diverted all but 3% of its waste away from landfills by applying circular economy reuse and upcycling practices to roughly 69% of manufacturing waste streams.

2026

Conserving water and resources

Intel expands water restoration and resource conservation programs across its manufacturing sites.

June 2026

Creating a more sustainable future

Named by TIME magazine as one of the World’s Most Sustainable Companies, acknowledging its success in balancing resource-heavy semiconductor fabrication.
